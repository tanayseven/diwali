Privacy Policy

Diwali Celebration · Last updated 14 November 2026

Diwali Celebration is a game about celebrating Diwali on a house's porch at night. This policy covers every version of it: the website at diwali.tanay.tech, the Android app (tech.tanay.diwali), and the desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux. It's made by Tanay PrabhuDesai, who is responsible for it ("I" below).

In short: the game doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party services.

What the game collects

Nothing. It doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it doesn't send anything you do in it anywhere. Everything you light, set off, hang or draw stays in the game on your device, and is gone when you close it.

What's kept on your device

Three settings are remembered on your device, so they're the same next time:

  • the language you picked,
  • the music's volume, and whether it's muted, and
  • which tools you've already been shown how to start with, so it's only shown once.

They're kept in your browser's or the app's local storage, are never sent to me or anyone else, and are deleted when you clear the site's data or the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The game uses no cookies.

Photos

The camera button takes a photo of the scene. The photo is made on your device and isn't uploaded anywhere. It's only shared with the app you pick from your device's share sheet, or saved where you choose. Once you share it, that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Permissions

The Android app's only permission is to vibrate, which Android grants without asking, so it's felt as things are put down and fireworks go off. It doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't send or load anything over the internet: its images, sounds and fonts are all part of the app, and it works offline. This policy is part of the app too. Other links in it, like the one to the author's blog, open in your browser.

The website's hosting

The website is hosted on Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3 and CloudFront). Like any web server, AWS may log the requests your browser makes to load the site, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, to deliver it and keep it secure. I don't use these logs to identify or track anyone. AWS handles them under the AWS Privacy Notice. The Android and desktop apps don't load anything from the website.

Sharing

As no personal data is collected, none is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone, including children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Links

The game links to the author's blog at blog.tanay.tech and to this policy. Other sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
