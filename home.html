Diwali Celebration

A house's porch on Diwali night, lit only by what you light on it. Light diyas, set off fireworks, hang fairy lights and kandils, and draw a rangoli.

A night on the porch

What's in it

  • 22 languages. Assamese to Urdu, each in its own script, Hinglish and Sanskrit included.
  • Fireworks over the city. Shells burst over a skyline strung with twinkling lights.
  • Akash kandils. Make paper lanterns, light them and hang them from the eaves.
  • Wind. Petals drift past, lanterns swing and smoke blows across the porch.
  • Sound all around. Every crackle and bang is heard from where it is, best with headphones.
  • Photos. Take a picture of your porch and share it.
  • Private. No ads, no accounts and no tracking: it collects nothing.
  • Free and open source. MIT-licensed, with the code on GitHub.
